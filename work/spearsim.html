SPEARSIM.

Privacy-minded phishing simulations with follow-up training.

A phishing-simulation platform for security-awareness programmes. Employees are imported from a CSV checked against the organisation's domain and given pseudonymous aliases, so the LLM (Groq) only ever sees an alias and a role, never a name or address. An admin must record consent before a campaign can launch. Emails go out through SendGrid, opens and clicks are tracked by UUID, and anyone caught gets a short awareness quiz. It also has role-based access, audit logging, erasure by alias and automatic deletion of campaign data after 90 days.

What I built

  • Designed the privacy model: domain-checked CSV import, pseudonymous aliases, and LLM prompts that carry only an alias and a role.
  • Built Supabase auth with three roles, a consent gate before launch, audit logging, erasure by alias and 90-day retention cleanup.
  • Built the email pipeline (Groq generation and SendGrid delivery through Celery, with an inline fallback), the tracking endpoints and the awareness quiz.
