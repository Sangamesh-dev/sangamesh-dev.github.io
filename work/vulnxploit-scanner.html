VULNXPLOIT SCANNER.

A small web front end for nmap service scans.

A Flask app with a vanilla-JavaScript front end. It runs nmap against 12 common ports, looks each detected service up in a small built-in table of known CVEs (SSH, HTTP and MySQL), shows a proof-of-concept template for those entries and exports results as a PDF or CSV. It's deliberately simple: the CVE table is built in, and the proof-of-concept templates are text, not exploits.
