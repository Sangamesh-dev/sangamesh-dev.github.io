MAILGUARD AI.

Gmail impersonation detection with SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks.

A Chrome extension for Gmail that flags likely impersonation. It pairs a stylometric (writing-style) classifier, trained on the Enron email corpus, with real-time SPF, DKIM and DMARC validation. Backend in FastAPI.
